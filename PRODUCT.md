## Product Purpose
Explain Deep Air Cool Solutions' services and turn local visits into immediate phone calls or service requests.

Spanish is the default language and English is fully available. Calling is the primary action. Preserve the verified phone number (305) 481-2522 and contact@deepaircool.com. Do not fabricate commercial claims, testimonials, addresses, or project details.

## Brand Commitments
Keep the Deep Air Cool Solutions name, supplied logo, recognizable blue/cyan identity, and real supplied field photographs. The supplied van photograph must lead the hero.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing website at https://deepaircool.com/ lists services, phone, email, service areas, hours, and emergency service. The repo contains a logo, eleven photographs, and a video. The exact names and stories of photographed projects are not supplied.

## Product Principles
- Make the call action obvious and usable on mobile.
- Show real work before making claims.
- Keep Spanish and English content equally complete.
- Use local facts without creating doorway pages or invented proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive layout, keyboard access, readable contrast, reduced motion support, and a clearly exposed language switch.
